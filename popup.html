Why this works

The problem

😴

You restart the router

Airtel or Jio Wi‑Fi stalls. You turn the router off and on, pages load again, so you keep doing that walk.

🗺

Restart only wakes Wi‑Fi

The link went idle. A restart wakes it. This app does that wake-up for you and leaves the router alone.

What you get

🏗

Hammer or let it run

Tap Hammer Hard when a call stalls, or leave auto wakes on. Same wake-up, no trip to the router.

🚦

See the last 24 hours

The chart shows auto wakes. Hammer Hard wakes harder when stuck and can use more data.

About the speeds

📏

Auto wake is a light check, so low speeds are normal

It moves only a tiny amount of data (about 128 KB down, 64 KB up) just to wake Wi‑Fi, so the numbers read low. Hammer Hard runs a full speed test for real numbers.

Good to know

  • Skip the router restart
  • Auto wake every 5 minutes to 6 hours
  • Pause anytime
  • History stays on this laptop only
  • Hammer Hard stays off this chart
  • Failed wakes show up here

This does not make your Airtel or Jio plan faster — it only wakes stuck Wi‑Fi.
