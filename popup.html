Why this?

The problem

😴

You turn the router off and on

Airtel or Jio WiFi gets stuck. You switch the router off and on. The page opens. It works, so you keep doing it.

🗺

That only wakes the WiFi

The WiFi went lazy. The restart woke it up. You can hammer it instead and leave the router on.

What you get

🏗

Just hammer hard

Hit Hammer Hard, or let it run every few minutes. The next page opens. No walk to the router.

🚦

See the whole day

Look at the last 24 hours. Was WiFi ok, or was it lazy? Hammer Hard checks real speed. It can eat a lot of your data.

Good to know

  • No need to turn the router off and on
  • Pick how often, from 5 minutes to 6 hours
  • Pause it anytime
  • Today's record stays on this laptop only
  • Speed test numbers stay off the daily chart
  • If a check fails, you will see it

This does not make your Airtel or Jio plan faster. It depends on your WiFi and router.
